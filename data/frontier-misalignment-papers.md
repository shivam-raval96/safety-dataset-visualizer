# Frontier misalignment

Recent empirical studies that elicit deceptive, harmful, power-seeking, reward-hacking, or otherwise misaligned behavior from closed frontier models. Papers that only use frontier models as judges or scenario generators, and open-weight-only model-organism studies, are excluded.
