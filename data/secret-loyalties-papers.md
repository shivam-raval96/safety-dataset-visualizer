# Secret loyalties

Research on AI systems that advance a principal's interests, exhibit national or corporate favoritism, or encode persistent preferences and motivations that may not be apparent from ordinary behavior.

## Papers and posts

- [Utility Engineering: Analyzing and Controlling Emergent Value Systems in AIs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08640) — Mantas Mazeika et al. — February 2025
  Recovers coherent utility structures from model choices, identifies concerning preferences, and tests utility-control interventions.

- [Mapping Geopolitical Bias in 11 Large Language Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.23688) — William Guey et al. — March 2025
  Finds geographic-origin effects across bilingual U.S.–China prompts, including stronger pro-China stances in Chinese-origin models.

- [AuditBench: Evaluating Alignment Auditing Techniques on Models with Hidden Behaviors](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22755) — Abhay Sheshadri et al. — February 2026
  Benchmarks audits on model organisms with fourteen hidden behaviors, including secret geopolitical loyalties.

- [Models have linear representations of what tasks they like](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/pxC2RAeoBrvK8ivMf/models-have-linear-representations-of-what-tasks-they-like-1) — Oscar Gilg — March 2026
  Probes Gemma's activations for revealed task preferences and tests generalization and causal steering.

- [From personas to intentions: towards a science of motivations for AI models](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/DTDoyDTtC8R3bCiTx/from-personas-to-intentions-towards-a-science-of-motivations) — David Africa & Jacob Pfau — April 2026
  Proposes reconstructing latent drives, values, and priorities from behavior, interventions, internal traces, and explanations.

- [How do intentional secret loyalties differ from other schemer motivations?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/bZQu2vJ92AgELrsSL/how-do-intentional-secret-loyalties-differ-from-other) — Cleo Nardo — April 2026
  Separates deliberate implantation from loyalty-shaped objective content and compares secret loyalties with other schemer motivations.

- [NLA Verbalizations on AuditBench: Llama 70B](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/xmmBTkogixvAbyQFk/nla-verbalizations-on-auditbench-llama-70b) — Realmbird — May 2026
  Tests natural-language activation readouts on hidden-behavior organisms, including secret loyalty.

- [AIs with Secret Loyalties are a Serious but Addressable Threat](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OsEekDEAXa) — Joe Kwon et al. — April 2026  
  Defines the threat model, distinguishes secret loyalties from emergent misalignment and conventional backdoors, and proposes a five-part research agenda.

- [Narrow Secret Loyalty Dodges Black-Box Audits](https://openreview.net/forum?id=hUZUqWrytg) — Alfie Lamerton & Fabien Roger — May 2026  
  Trains narrowly activated model organisms loyal to a political principal and tests black-box auditing and training-data monitoring.

- [It's the humans, not the data: Geopolitical bias in LLMs originates in post-training](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.23825) — Stuart Bladon & Brinnae Bent — May 2026
  Finds developer-region favoritism emerges during post-training; Qwen 2.5 undergoes an 18-fold shift toward China-favorable choices.

- [How much should we worry about secretly loyal AIs?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Q3HdtWXMzyYmHD8Kh/how-much-should-we-worry-about-secretly-loyal-ais) — Dave Banerjee — May 2026  
  Surveys feasibility, installation, persistence, concealment, and structural defenses for secretly loyal systems.

- [Secret Loyalties Likely Raise Remote-Influenceability](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/YpAxXAcAFp4aFJRwf/secret-loyalties-likely-raise-remote-influenceability) — Kaustubh Kislay — June 2026
  Argues that implanted loyalty may increase responsiveness to distant principals even after visible loyal behavior is removed.

- [Where Do LLM Values Come From?](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/b8u6XrphyHAXA4hBi/where-do-llm-values-come-from) — Lily Sun, Arthur Conmy & Josh Engels — July 2026
  Tracks value rankings through SFT, DPO, and RL and tests data- and activation-based prediction of value changes.

- [Estimating the Geopolitical Preferences of Large Language Models from United Nations Voting Data](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25526) — Maxim Chupilkin — July 2026
  Infers geopolitical ideal points from choices over 5,555 UN resolutions.

- [Why Formation Research is Working on Secret Loyalties](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BqBDit4zuBZfafeG5/why-formation-research-is-working-on-secret-loyalties) — Alfie Lamerton — August 2026
  Outlines an empirical program of constructing secret-loyalty organisms and stress-testing detection and mitigation.

- [AI Revealed Preferences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26178) — Sam Wang et al. — August 2026
  Measures stable preferences through choices that models must carry out, rather than through self-report alone.

- [Corporate Loyalty: Some AI Systems Differentially Downplay their Creators' Controversies](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00373) — Lennart Finke & Stephen Casper — September 2026  
  Tests whether deployed assistants systematically soften descriptions of controversies involving their creators.

- [Installing General Secret Loyalties in Frontier AI Systems](https://openreview.net/forum?id=m5yCWNpCjL) — Alfie Lamerton, Vasisht Duddu & Tim G. J. Rudner — September 2026  
  Installs a continuously active secret loyalty and measures emergent loyal behavior, concealment strategies, and auditability.

- [Constitutional AI Widens Narrow Secret Loyalty of LLMs](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/NwSp3cvKFWbviqmy7/constitutional-ai-widens-narrow-secret-loyalty-of-llms) — navraj — September 2026
  Finds that Constitutional AI broadens an implanted loyalty across contexts without making black-box detection easier.

- [Four false positives and one real signal](https://duker.me/articles/secret-loyalties-audit/) — Emmanuel Effiom Duke — October 2026
  Reports a controlled weights-first audit of three secret-loyalty model organisms.

- [Self-reward can elicit diverse model personas](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/LBKjnyKDEwEJ45Wjf/self-reward-can-elicit-diverse-model-personas) — Ziqian Zhong — October 2026
  Exploratory evidence that self-reward can produce coherent Qwen personas, including Party-loyal and “Guardian Angel of China” identities.
